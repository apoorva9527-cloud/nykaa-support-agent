# International Shipping Restrictions

> Demo policy created only for the Nykaa Support Agent project. It does not represent Nykaa's real policy.

## Shipping availability

- This demo service supports delivery only within India.
- Orders cannot be shipped to international addresses.
- Customers must use a valid Indian delivery address and postal code.

## Restricted items

- Some beauty and electronic products may also have shipping restrictions because of safety, battery, or regulatory requirements.
- Availability can vary by delivery location and product category.

## Support guidance

- Clearly explain that international delivery is not available in this demo policy.
- Do not promise exceptions without confirmation from the operations team.
- For address changes, check whether the order is still in the Placed status before offering help.
