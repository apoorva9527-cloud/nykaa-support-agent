# Payment Failure and Retry Policy

> Demo policy created only for the Nykaa Support Agent project. It does not represent Nykaa's real policy.

## Common reasons for payment failure

- Insufficient account balance.
- Incorrect card or UPI details.
- Bank-side decline or temporary outage.
- Expired payment session.
- Network interruption during checkout.

## What the customer can do

- Confirm that the payment details are correct.
- Try the payment again after a few minutes.
- Use another supported payment method if the problem continues.
- Do not retry repeatedly if an amount appears to be debited.

## Support guidance

- Never ask for card PIN, CVV, UPI PIN, or OTP.
- If money was debited but no order was created, advise the customer that a reversal may take 3 to 5 business days.
- Escalate transactions that are not reversed after the expected time.
