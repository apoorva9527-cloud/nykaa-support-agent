# Delivery Service-Level Agreement

> Demo policy created only for the Nykaa Support Agent project. It does not represent Nykaa's real policy.

## Expected delivery timelines

| Delivery location | Expected timeline |
|---|---:|
| Metro cities | 2 to 4 business days |
| Other cities | 4 to 7 business days |
| Remote locations | 7 to 10 business days |

## Delivery updates

- An order is marked as shipped when it is handed to the delivery partner.
- Customers receive tracking details after shipment.
- Delivery dates are estimates and may change because of weather, holidays, or operational delays.
- A shipment is considered delayed when it is beyond the expected delivery window.

## Support guidance

- Check the order ID and order status before providing an update.
- For a delayed shipment, apologise and share the latest available tracking status.
- Escalate if there has been no tracking update for 72 hours after shipment.
