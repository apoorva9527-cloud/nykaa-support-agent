# Loyalty Points Policy

> Demo policy created only for the Nykaa Support Agent project. It does not represent Nykaa's real policy.

## Earning points

- Customers may earn loyalty points on eligible purchases.
- Points are credited after the order is delivered.
- Cancelled, returned, or refunded orders do not earn loyalty points.
- The number of points earned may depend on promotions and product eligibility.

## Redeeming points

- Points can be used only on eligible products and orders.
- The available balance is shown at checkout.
- Points cannot be exchanged for cash.
- Points may expire according to the validity date shown in the customer account.

## Support guidance

- Verify the customer account and order ID before discussing points.
- If points are missing after delivery, allow up to 48 hours for processing before escalation.
