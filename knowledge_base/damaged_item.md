# Damaged or Incorrect Item Claim Policy

> Demo policy created only for the Nykaa Support Agent project. It does not represent Nykaa's real policy.

## Claim requirements

- A damaged, defective, missing, or incorrect item must be reported within 48 hours of delivery.
- The customer should provide the order ID and clear photos or an unboxing video when available.
- The item, packaging, invoice, and accessories should be retained until the claim is resolved.

## Resolution process

1. Support records the claim and verifies the order details.
2. Evidence is reviewed for eligibility.
3. An approved claim may result in replacement, return pickup, or refund.
4. The customer receives updates through the registered contact details.

## Support guidance

- Do not blame the customer before the claim has been reviewed.
- Prioritise claims involving a wrong product, a missing item, or transit damage.
- Escalate unresolved claims after 3 business days.
