# Customer Support Escalation Matrix

> Demo policy created only for the Nykaa Support Agent project. It does not represent Nykaa's real policy.

## Level 1: Standard support

Handle routine questions about delivery estimates, return eligibility, order status, cancellations, and loyalty points.

## Level 2: Specialist support

Escalate when:

- A refund has not arrived after the documented timeline.
- A shipment has no tracking update for more than 72 hours.
- A valid return, exchange, or warranty claim was rejected.
- A payment was debited but not reversed within 5 business days.

## Level 3: Urgent escalation

Escalate immediately when:

- There is suspected account fraud or an unauthorised transaction.
- A customer reports a safety issue with a product.
- Personal data or payment information may be exposed.
- The customer has an unresolved issue after Level 2 review.

## Safety rule

Support agents must never ask for passwords, OTPs, card PINs, CVVs, or UPI PINs.
