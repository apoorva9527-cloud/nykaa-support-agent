# Warranty Terms

> Demo policy created only for the Nykaa Support Agent project. It does not represent Nykaa's real policy.

## Warranty coverage

- Warranty applies only to eligible electronics and selected home appliances.
- The warranty period is shown on the product page and invoice.
- The original invoice is required to raise a warranty claim.
- Warranty covers manufacturing defects only.

## Not covered

- Physical damage caused after delivery.
- Damage caused by water, misuse, or unauthorised repair.
- Consumable parts and normal wear and tear.
- Missing accessories that were not reported at delivery.

## Support guidance

- Verify the order ID, product category, and invoice date.
- Direct eligible customers to the authorised service centre or warranty-claim process.
- Escalate when a valid warranty claim is rejected without a clear reason.
