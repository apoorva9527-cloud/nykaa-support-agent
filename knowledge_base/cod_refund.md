# Cash on Delivery Refund Policy

> Demo policy created only for the Nykaa Support Agent project. It does not represent Nykaa's real policy.

## Refund timelines

- For cancelled COD orders, no refund is needed because payment was not collected.
- For a returned COD order, the refund is processed after the returned item passes quality checks.
- Refunds are issued to the bank account or UPI details provided by the customer.
- Processing usually takes 5 to 7 business days after the quality check is complete.
- The customer receives a confirmation once the refund has been initiated.

## Support guidance

- Verify the order ID before discussing refund information.
- If the refund has not arrived after 7 business days, create a support escalation.
- Do not ask the customer to share OTP, card PIN, or UPI PIN.
